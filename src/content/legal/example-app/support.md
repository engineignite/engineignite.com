Email **go@engineignite.com** and we will get back to you within two business days.

## Before you write

Including these details usually gets an answer in one round trip:

- Your device and OS version (Settings → General → About).
- The app version (shown on the app's Settings screen).
- What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
