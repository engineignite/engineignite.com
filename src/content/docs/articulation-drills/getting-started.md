A session is three to five minutes. One sound set, then it stops. Short and
repeatable works better than long and once.

## Pick a sound set

Sound sets are organised the way therapy is planned: by sound, and by where the
sound sits in the word. Beginning /s/ and ending /s/ are separate sets, because
they are separate skills.

Pick the set the child is currently working on. If a speech-language pathologist
assigned a target sound, that is the set to open.

## Work the deck

Each card shows a picture and plays the word, spoken by a child's voice. The
child listens, then repeats it back. Swipe or tap to move to the next card.

The deck is the whole set, shuffled fresh each time, so the order never becomes
something the child memorises instead of the sound.

## Finish

The deck ends on a celebration screen. Stop there. The app has no streaks, no
timers and no way to lose. Nothing here turns practice into pressure.

## Settings worth knowing

- **Printed words** can be shown or hidden on the cards, depending on whether the
  child is reading yet.
- **Restore purchases** re-unlocks the full set of sounds on a new device.
